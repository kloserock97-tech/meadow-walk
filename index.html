Meadow Walk

An endless, unhurried flight over a windy meadow. Half a million blades of grass are placed and bent on the GPU, and the cursor sends soft waves through the field that lift pebbles and dust before letting them settle.

H hide the panel · Space freeze time · M wind sound · move the cursor over the grass
